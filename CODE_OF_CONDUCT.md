## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- respectful, constructive feedback
- focus on what is best for clarity and engineering quality
- accepting responsibility and apologizing for mistakes
- empathy toward other community members

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes
- publishing others’ private information
- sustained disruption of discussions
- sexualized language or imagery in project spaces

## Enforcement

Report incidents to `contato@forge.michaelmachado.dev`.

The maintainer will review and may take any action deemed appropriate, including warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban from project spaces.
